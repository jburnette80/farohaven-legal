Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Farohaven Bookkeeping application (the "Application"), operated by Farohaven LLC, handles information when used by authorized Farohaven LLC operators.

1. Information the Application Accesses

Depending on the features enabled by an authorized operator, the Application may access:

  • QuickBooks Online data, including company, account, transaction, deposit, purchase, and related accounting information needed to perform bookkeeping workflows.
  • Gmail data through read-only access used to identify and interpret relevant business emails such as Airbnb payout notices and purchase receipts.
  • Google Sheets data used for designated business expense tracking. The Application writes to a designated sheet only when an authorized operator explicitly requests that action.
  • Local bookkeeping data, including reconciliation status, review decisions, account mappings, transaction metadata, and audit information used by the Application.

2. How Information Is Used

Information is used only to support Farohaven LLC's internal bookkeeping and accounting workflows, including reconciling payouts, identifying receipts, categorizing expenses, preparing accounting entries, preventing duplicate postings, and maintaining bookkeeping records.

3. Human Approval of Financial Actions

The Application is designed so that accounting writes are subject to authorized-user review and approval. Google Sheets expense-tracker writes are not enabled by default and occur only when specifically requested for the applicable transaction.

4. Storage

The Application is designed primarily for local operation. Application data, OAuth tokens, and service connection files may be stored on the authorized operator's local computer. Source code repositories are configured so that local secrets, tokens, and bookkeeping database files are not intentionally committed to source control.

5. Sharing and Sale of Data

Farohaven LLC does not sell, rent, or use Application data for advertising. Information may be transmitted to connected service providers, such as Intuit or Google, only as necessary to perform actions authorized through those services.

6. Third-Party Services

Connected services process information under their own privacy policies and terms. The Application's use of Google user data is limited to the functionality described in this policy and the permissions granted by the authorized Google account.

7. Retention and Deletion

Local Application data is retained as needed for bookkeeping, audit, and operational purposes until deleted by an authorized operator. Access to Google or Intuit services can be revoked through the applicable account settings, and locally stored connection tokens can be deleted from the authorized device.

8. Security

Farohaven LLC uses reasonable administrative and technical safeguards appropriate for an internal bookkeeping application, including local credential storage and restricted source-control handling of secrets. No system can guarantee absolute security.

9. Scope

The Application is an internal business tool and is not offered as a consumer service. It is not intended for use by children.

10. Changes

Farohaven LLC may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date above will be revised when material changes are made.

11. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy may be directed to Farohaven LLC through its ordinary business contact channels.

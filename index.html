Farohaven Bookkeeping

Farohaven Bookkeeping is an internal bookkeeping automation application used by Farohaven LLC and its authorized operators.

The application connects to approved third-party services such as QuickBooks Online, Gmail, and Google Sheets to assist with bookkeeping workflows, including payout reconciliation, expense review, and approved accounting entries.
